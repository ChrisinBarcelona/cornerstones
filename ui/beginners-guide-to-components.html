Welcome to the world of components, where efficiency meets creativity! In this step-by-step guide, from components list → components → library = high fidelity.

This process is all about productivity. Using components to build a comprehensive design system streamlines your workflow inexorably and enhances the overall design thinking process by allowing you to quickly pivot away from a design decision and introduce a new module into your UI when necessary.
