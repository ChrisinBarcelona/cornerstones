# Sprint 01: Honest Greens

![A salad bowl of leaves, cucumber and broccoli beside a halved avocado on a wooden table](honest-greens.jpg)

> A clear service problem that needs a redesign.

## The brief

Honest Greens is a busy healthy-food restaurant chain in Barcelona. At lunchtime, hundreds of people need to order, pay, wait, collect, find a seat and get back to work, all in a short break. Your job is to go into a real restaurant, map how the service actually works today, find where it breaks down, and redesign it.

This is a **service design** sprint. You are designing the whole experience, not just a screen: the people, the space, the technology and the processes behind the counter.

## The problem

Fast, healthy food only works if the service is fast too. During the lunchtime peak, small frictions add up: unclear ordering, queues, waiting without knowing how long, a crowded collection point, nowhere to sit. Nobody has mapped what really happens from the customer's side *and* from behind the counter, so nobody knows where the real problems are.

## Your challenge

**How might we** make the Honest Greens lunchtime experience feel as fresh and effortless as the food, for customers and staff alike?

## Sprint goals

- Observe the service as it really works today, at peak time.
- Map the customer's journey and the service behind it.
- Find the moments that cause the most frustration, and their causes.
- Learn from how other services solve similar problems.
- Set a visual direction for the redesign.
- Design and test a better service with real people, in public.

## Scope

**In scope**
- The in-restaurant experience at lunchtime: arriving, ordering, paying, waiting, collecting, eating and leaving.
- The digital touchpoints that support it (kiosk, app, screens, notifications).
- What staff do frontstage and backstage to deliver it.

**Out of scope**
- The menu, recipes and prices.
- Delivery-only orders and partner delivery apps.
- Rebranding Honest Greens. You may set a visual direction, but the brand stays theirs.

## Deliverables

1. **Site visit notes**: observations, photos and sketches from a real restaurant at peak time.
2. **As-Is customer journey map** of today's experience.
3. **Service design blueprint** in the FigJam template.
4. **Indirect benchmark** of three services outside food that solve a similar problem well.
5. **Mood board and brand pack** for your redesign.
6. **Paper, mid-fi and hi-fi prototypes** of your key touchpoints.
7. **Usability-testing findings**, synthesised into themes.
8. **A general-audience presentation** of your redesign.

## Constraints and tips

- **Go at peak time.** 13:30 on a weekday tells you far more than 11:00 on a Monday.
- **Be a respectful guest.** Buy something, don't get in the staff's way, and never photograph people without consent.
- **Look behind the counter.** The cause of a customer's frustration is often backstage.
- **Test in public.** Week 3 ends with real prospective users, not classmates.
- This is a speculative student project: Honest Greens has not commissioned it.

## Success looks like

A redesign backed by evidence from a real restaurant: you can point to the exact moment in the journey that fails, show its backstage cause on your blueprint, and show tested screens or touchpoints that fix it.
