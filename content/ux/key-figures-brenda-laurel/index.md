## Who they are

Brenda Laurel is an American interaction designer, game designer and researcher with a PhD in theatre. She worked at Atari, was a pioneer in developing virtual reality and co-founded the game studio Purple Moon. She later founded design graduate programmes at Art Center College of Design and California College of the Arts.

## Key contributions

- ***Computers as Theatre* (1991):** she argued that an interface is like a stage, where the user is an actor taking part in the action, not just an audience.
- **Purple Moon (1996):** games designed for girls, based on extensive research with them, at a time when games were made almost only for boys.
- **Virtual reality:** early research into how people experience immersive worlds.

## Why it matters for UX

Laurel brought drama, narrative and research with real users into interaction design. Purple Moon is a classic example of designing for an overlooked audience by actually listening to them, and her ideas about immersion are more relevant than ever in spatial computing.
