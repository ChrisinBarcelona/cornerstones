# Key Figures: Daniel Kahneman

Daniel Kahneman is an Israeli-American psychologist and economist who is renowned for his groundbreaking work in the field of behavioral economics

![Daniel_Kahneman_(3283955327)_(cropped).jpg](Daniel_Kahneman_-3283955327-_-cropped-.jpg)

Kahneman's research has had a profound impact on our understanding of human decision-making and judgment. HIs work demonstrated that people often make decisions based on heuristics (mental shortcuts) and are influenced by various cognitive biases, rather than strictly following rational and logical reasoning.

Field: **Psychology**

Major Contribution: His work challenged the prevailing assumption in traditional economics that individuals always make rational and optimal decisions.

Worked: University of British Columbia

Era: 1970

Best Quote: "The idea that the future is unpredictable is undermined every day by the ease with which the past is explained.”
