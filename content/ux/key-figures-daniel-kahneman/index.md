## Who they are

Kahneman was an Israeli-American psychologist. With Amos Tversky he spent decades studying how people really make judgements and decisions, and his work helped create the field of behavioural economics.

## Key contributions

- **Heuristics and biases:** people rely on mental shortcuts that lead to predictable errors, such as anchoring and availability.
- **Prospect theory (1979):** losses feel bigger than equal gains (loss aversion).
- **System 1 and System 2:** fast, automatic thinking versus slow, effortful thinking.

## Why it matters for UX

Most of the time, users run on System 1: fast, intuitive and easily biased. Designing for that means clear defaults, simple choices and honest framing. His work is the basis of the Bias lectures, and a reminder to use this knowledge ethically, never to manipulate.
