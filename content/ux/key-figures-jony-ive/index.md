# Key Figures: Jony Ive

Jony Ive played a pivotal role in shaping the aesthetic and functional design of numerous iconic Apple products, including the iMac, iPod, iPhone, and iPad. 

![12APPLE-videoSixteenByNine3000.jpg](12APPLE-videoSixteenByNine3000.jpg)

Jonathan Ive's biggest contribution to Apple was his transformative impact on the company's product design language and the overall user experience. His design philosophy and approach revolutionized the way technology products were perceived and experienced by users. He championed sleek, minimalist designs that emphasized clean lines, simplicity, and a focus on the user experience. His designs brought a sense of elegance and sophistication to technology products, setting them apart from the competition.

Field: **Industrial Design**

Major Contribution: iPhone

Worked: Apple

Era: 1995

Best Quote: "Simplicity is not the absence of clutter; it's the absence of complexity.”
