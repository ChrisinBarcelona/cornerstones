## Who they are

Ive is a British industrial designer. He joined Apple in 1992, led its design team and became Chief Design Officer, before leaving in 2019 to found the design collective LoveFrom.

## Key contributions

- **The iMac G3 (1998):** a colourful, friendly computer that helped turn Apple around.
- **The iPod, iPhone and iPad:** products defined by simplicity, with as few buttons and controls as possible.
- **Hardware and software together:** he also led Apple's interface design, including the flat redesign of iOS 7.

## Why it matters for UX

Ive's work shows how much the physical and the digital experience belong together, and how simplicity takes enormous care. His focus on removing everything that isn't needed is a lesson in the aesthetic and minimalist design heuristic.
