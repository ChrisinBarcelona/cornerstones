# Key Figures: Tim Brown

Tim Brown is a prominent designer and former CEO of IDEO, a renowned design and innovation consultancy. 

![1654102255-brown_tim_headshot_new.jpg](1654102255-brown_tim_headshot_new.jpg)

Brown is a strong advocate for the integration of design thinking into various aspects of business and society. He believes that design can drive positive change and innovation by empathizing with users, exploring diverse perspectives, and embracing experimentation and iteration.

Field: **Industrial Design**

Major Contribution: Design Thinking

Worked: Ideo Design 

Era: 1987

Best Quote: "Design thinking is a human-centered approach to innovation that draws from the designer's toolkit to integrate the needs of people, the possibilities of technology, and the requirements for business success.”
