## Who they are

Brown is a British industrial designer who led the design and innovation consultancy IDEO as its CEO for almost two decades. He became one of the best-known advocates of design thinking in business and society.

## Key contributions

- **Design thinking:** a human-centred, iterative approach to problem solving that anyone in an organisation can use.
- **Desirability, viability and feasibility:** the IDEO balance at the heart of the DVF analysis.
- **Design for social impact:** applying design thinking beyond products, to services, organisations and social problems.

## Why it matters for UX

The design-thinking process you follow in your sprints, from empathy to testing, owes much to IDEO and to Brown's books and talks. The DVF analysis method comes directly from this tradition.
