# Design Origins

## Origins of UX and The Birth of The Internet.

Whilst there is a very formal understanding of UX as a series of applied methods that designers use to make software easy, accessible and fun to use, what UX Design actually is, is a massive combination of factors. UX is a discipline, a philosophy, a natural attention to detail, it’s emotional design, an entire service journey, it’s an in depth understanding the user but in truth, it’s all these things and more.

---

We’ll start with a super brief introduction into where UX started (sort of) thereafter discuss the internet as much of our work takes place there these days.

---

UX Design and design in general comes from controlling the natural world around us so we are in the process of invention. of inventing tools. Tools to solve problems and hopefully this first example will really drive home that sense of necessity to crisis that is usually at the heart of innovation. In the modern world, it’s easy to create for the sake of creating. Now, more than ever, with the advent of AI. However, it’s vital to create for purpose. This is a concept that not only lies at the heart of technology but at the heart of good design. just need to know that all design is user experience design, just if it’s either a good experience or a bad experience.

## The first designer

Good UX design is a story, so let's tell one. Really the very first UX designer was probably Homo Habilis. Let's cast our minds back about 3.3 million years ago to the early Pleistocene. Here we have him coming up with a solution to a problem. Homo Habilis wants to kills deer. He can’t. he needs a tool. He is going to build a hafted spear so he is sharpening a stone. 

![chriskellylive_close_up_of_a_cavemans_hands_as_he_is_sharpening_12c8858e-13aa-426d-b37a-d97bff5b5e3f.png](chriskellylive_close_up_of_a_cavemans_hands_as_he_is_sharpening_12c8858e-13aa-426d-b37a-d97bff5b5e3f.jpg)

Homo Habilis is now engaged with balancing three primal ideas concerning the tip of the spear which he wants to make.

- Stone has to be **sharp enough**
- Stone has to be **light enough to carry**
- Stone to be **strong enough**

---

![photo_1353010363546-1-0.jpg](photo_1353010363546-1-0.jpg)

This is essential. Without these three properties. The spear would not work. These translate naturally into three properties which we still use today:

- Functionality - What it does.
- Accessibility - Where it is done.
- Consistency - How often it can be done.

---

Our ancient relative must have engaged in some **advanced thinking and planning** to make that spear! More over, archeologists recognise the fact that, since these spears were so widely found throughout the Middle Palaeolithic era, early hominids must have talked to each other. Unfortunately it didn’t work out for him. The spear lacked functionality. Homo Habilis is hearing feedback from his family but he’s finding the news a little hard to accept. You on the other hand must learn to take design critic well.

## Byzantine Art

Consistency is key. Beauty is in the eye of the beholder. Let's visit the eastern world for a moment to visit the intense gorgeous visualisation of the Byzantine empire. What we see here is the emergence of a thematic and visual preoccupation. With intense mosaics on tile, decorative golds and stylized perceptions, we see a drift away from natural form into a world within a world, in your work, you will create a realm all of its own. A system which speaks for itself. An entire system. A design system, with its own style entirely. Envisage an entire ecosystem with an ecosystem. A world within a world.

## Birth of the Internet

![chriskellylive_a_cinematic_1960s_scene_as_a_bunch_of_engineers__43fbbdd2-65dc-4476-b003-66c2c1a9f5e4.png](chriskellylive_a_cinematic_1960s_scene_as_a_bunch_of_engineers__43fbbdd2-65dc-4476-b003-66c2c1a9f5e4.jpg)

Although the first internet (or intranet) was invented in the 1960's in the form of a peer to peer file sharing network used by government departments in the United States, it wasn't until January 1, 1983 that we actually saw the rise of what we understand today as the global internet.

![chriskellylive_make_me_a_cinematic_photo_scene_from_1982._Make__5ae6a82e-71f2-4ea0-ab8c-43ed028cd229.png](chriskellylive_make_me_a_cinematic_photo_scene_from_1982._Make__5ae6a82e-71f2-4ea0-ab8c-43ed028cd229.jpg)

---

Consecutively, the birth of the internet was accompanied with the rise of the personal computer. around 1984. In the early days of the internet, there was no major emphasis put on the user because during this time, it was mostly engineers and technicians that were the users. What changed the world was the consecutive advent of the personal computer because now the computer was for everyone. 

![chriskellylive_gorgeous_image_rich_cinematic_product_photograph_c4a8c874-f5bf-4bda-ac99-2993d43c8cab.png](chriskellylive_gorgeous_image_rich_cinematic_product_photograph_c4a8c874-f5bf-4bda-ac99-2993d43c8cab.jpg)

Below is an image of the one of the first landing pages belonging the Internet Service Provider, Netscape in 1994. These early web pioneers didn’t really give much consideration to the User Experience. Whilst there was some basic styling, there was no css and the page rendered out in plain html (hyper text mark up language) and was accessible over 56k dial up connection.

![Screen-Shot-2015-08-03-at-10.webp](Screen-Shot-2015-08-03-at-10.webp)

It's at this point in the mid nineties, when the internet was gaining popularity, the conversation really begins around user experience.

![p-1-90176202-apples-advanced-technology-group-changed-the-world-1.jpg](p-1-90176202-apples-advanced-technology-group-changed-the-world-1.jpg)

In 1993, an engineer at Apple, Don Norman is given the job title, ‘user experience architect’. This was the first time that the phrase user experience was featured in a job description. 

![chriskellylive_scene_set_mid_nineties_young_handsome_engineer_o_7b7645bb-fba5-44e0-9a01-df553857039d.png](chriskellylive_scene_set_mid_nineties_young_handsome_engineer_o_7b7645bb-fba5-44e0-9a01-df553857039d.jpg)

Don Norman established a small, high-level group, called the ‘user experience architect's office’, which worked across the company to make Apple products **easier to use**. Today he is considered by many to be the father of UX. Let's stick with 1994 and California.

![chriskellylive_the_first_ever_landing_page_first_landing_pages__3c00be0a-5c7e-417c-8ca0-cfc6a3bc7d36.png](chriskellylive_the_first_ever_landing_page_first_landing_pages__3c00be0a-5c7e-417c-8ca0-cfc6a3bc7d36.jpg)

## Why it matters for UX

Knowing where UX comes from shows you that it is not a fashion or a job title but the oldest kind of design: making tools that fit people. The same three questions the first toolmakers faced, functionality, accessibility and consistency, are the ones you will ask of every screen you design.

## Try it: the history of one object (20 minutes)

1. Pick an everyday object, such as a chair, a phone or a pair of scissors.
2. Find two earlier versions of it (an image search is fine).
3. For each version, write what problem it solved better than the one before.
4. Rate each version for functionality, accessibility and consistency, and share the object's story with a classmate in two minutes.

- UX became a profession with personal computers and the web, and the term with Don Norman at Apple in 1993.
