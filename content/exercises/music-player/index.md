# Music Player

## The brief

This one helps you get your head around lists. You've just joined **Bop**, a new music streaming service that wants to rival Spotify's premium price. A user is out for a jog, bumps into an old friend and wants to pause the music to chat. What do they see when they look down? Design the player that lets them pause and play.

![Music Player](5107ca5a0a24a104ad4e1e343fea1b3d.webp)

Source: [https://dribbble.com/adommeti](https://dribbble.com/adommeti)

## Deliverable

- The now-playing screen in an **iPhone 16** frame (393 × 852) in Figma, on the 8-point grid, in its paused state.
- Extra challenge: the track list it opens from.

## Must include

1. Play / pause
2. Track details
3. Artist artwork
4. Progress or sound-wave bar

## Nice to have

- Track list
- Upcoming track
- Playlist

☐ Every *Must include* item is on the screen

☐ It's obvious at a glance that the music is paused

☐ Text is readable and colour contrast passes (4.5:1 for body text)

☐ Layers are named and grouped; repeated elements are components
