# Sign In

## The brief

The bedrock of good UI is understanding how data moves from state to state, and nothing shows that better than a sign-in page. Design an effective sign-in page or pop-up for **Nomad**, a community for finding people to travel the world with.

**Extra challenge:** follow the user through several screens: before, while and after they enter their details.

![Sign In](625ce9f7f3e1bedba7a573a0f8ef5fcf.webp)

Source: [https://dribbble.com/christinesoules](https://dribbble.com/christinesoules)

## Deliverable

- A sign-in screen and a create-account screen in an **iPhone 16** frame (393 × 852) in Figma, on the 8-point grid.
- Extra challenge: an error state and a success state.

## Must include

1. Email or username
2. Password with show / hide
3. Sign in button
4. Forgot password
5. Create new account

## Nice to have

- Sign in with social media
- Confirm password (on create account)
- Logo, title text and imagery
