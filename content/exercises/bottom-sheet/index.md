# Bottom Sheet

## The brief

This challenge is all about feedback after an action. You're part of the design team at **Neobank**, a digital bank. Design the bottom sheet that tells a user their money has been sent to a friend successfully.

![Bottom Sheet](WhatsApp_Image_2023-05-31_at_13.20.44.jpeg)

*Source not recorded.*

## Deliverable

- The bottom sheet over the transfer screen, in an **iPhone 16** frame (393 × 852) in Figma, on the 8-point grid.

## Must include

1. Amount transferred
2. Sender
3. Receiver
4. A clear way to close the sheet

## Nice to have

- Neobank logo
- Payment review or receipt
- Share receipt
