### 📝 Afternoon Project Challenge

**Task:**

Design an app idea (e.g., something with a social impact like *SafeWalk*).

**Steps:**

1. **Inspiration** – Explore [Google Stitch](https://stitch.withgoogle.com/) to see how AI can generate and support UI concepts.
2. **Screens** – Collect screen inspiration and **import it into Figma** as a starting point for your design.
3. **Prototype** – Develop a **high-fidelity prototype** in Figma.
4. **Test** – Share and test your prototype with fellow students. Gather quick feedback.

**Deliverable:**

- A high-fidelity Figma prototype with at least **one complete user flow** (from entry → core task → outcome).
- A short reflection (2–3 minutes verbal or written) on what worked well and what you’d improve.
