# UX x AI

## The brief

An afternoon challenge: design an app idea with a social impact, such as **SafeWalk**, an app that helps people get home safely at night. Use AI tools to speed you up, while the design decisions stay yours.

1. **Inspiration:** explore [Google Stitch](https://stitch.withgoogle.com/) to see how AI can generate and support UI concepts.
2. **Screens:** collect screen inspiration and import it into Figma as a starting point.
3. **Prototype:** build a high-fidelity prototype in Figma.
4. **Test:** share it with classmates and gather quick feedback.

![UX x AI](Google-Stitch-Beta-App-preview.webp)

Source: [https://stitch.withgoogle.com/](https://stitch.withgoogle.com/)

## Deliverable

- A high-fidelity Figma prototype in an **iPhone 16** frame (393 × 852) in Figma, on the 8-point grid, with at least one complete user flow (entry → core task → outcome).
- A short reflection (2–3 minutes, spoken or written) on what worked and what you'd improve.

## Must include

1. One complete, clickable user flow
2. Your own visual style, not the AI's default
3. Feedback from at least two classmates

## Nice to have

- A second flow
- An onboarding screen
- A note on which parts the AI generated

## Inspiration

Use the AI tools above as your inspiration, and record which ones you used.

## Time box

**Time box:** one afternoon (about 4 hours)

**Difficulty:** ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️

## Before you submit

☐ Frame is iPhone 16 (393 × 852) and spacing follows the 8-point grid

☐ Every *Must include* item is on the screen

☐ You can explain every design decision without saying "the AI did it"

☐ The flow works end to end in Figma's prototype mode

☐ Text is readable and colour contrast passes (4.5:1 for body text)

☐ Layers are named and grouped; repeated elements are components

☐ Shared the Figma link and a PNG export with your instructor
