# Make an AI Interface

## The brief

Design **Ada**, an AI study assistant that students talk to naturally. Think beyond a simple chat box: how can the design make the AI feel smart, human and useful, while still matching the mental models and expectations people already have from other chat apps?

![Make an AI Interface](Screenshot_2025-10-13_at_15.09.05.png)

*Source not recorded.*

## Deliverable

- Two screens in an **iPhone 16** frame (393 × 852) in Figma, on the 8-point grid: the empty state, and a conversation in progress.

## Must include

1. Text input field and send button
2. Conversation thread with distinct user and AI messages
3. "Thinking…" indicator
4. Empty state prompt (e.g. "Ask me anything…")

## Nice to have

- Voice input
- Upload or attach
- Quick action chips (e.g. "Summarise", "Explain more")
- Assistant avatar or icon
