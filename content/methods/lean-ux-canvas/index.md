# Lean UX Canvas

# 🪜 Stage: Discovery

First framing of the problem.

# 👋 Context

The **Lean UX Canvas** is a good place to start as it helps you generate ideas and aligns a wider audience on the problem your are solving, who it’s for, and why it matters. It’s lightweight, collaborative, and focused on quick learning and idea generation.

# 🎯 Goal / Learning Outcome

Students will be able to **complete the first 4 sections of a Lean UX Canvas** to clarify problem, users, and user value.

# 🛠️ Main Exercise

1. Review the 8 sections of the Lean UX Canvas.
2. For this activity, **focus on Sections 1–4 only**:
    - **Business Outcomes** – How will we measure success?
    - **Users** – Who experiences this problem?
        - **Business Problem** – What issue are we solving?
    - **User Outcomes & Benefits** – What value will they gain if we succeed?

# ⏱️ Estimated Duration

- 25–30 minutes

# 👉 Extra Notes

- Capture your **current assumptions** — they don’t have to be “right.”
- Keep it **concise and testable.**
- You’ll revisit and revise later as you gather evidence.

# 🏞️ Template

Fill in the boxes:

1. **Business Problem:** ___________________________
2. **Business Outcomes:** ___________________________
3. **Users:** ___________________________
4. **User Outcomes & Benefits:** ___________________________

# ⚡ Tips

- Avoid vague answers (e.g. “make users happy”). Be specific.
- Think in terms of **measurable outcomes**, not just outputs.
- Use language your **team and stakeholders** will easily understand.

# ✨ AI Assist

Use AI to:

- Reframe your **Business Outcomes** as measurable metrics.
- Suggest alternative **User Outcomes & Benefits** based on your draft.

# ✅ Checklist

- [ ]  Business Problem is **clear and specific**
- [ ]  Business Outcomes are **measurable**
- [ ]  Users are **defined, not generic**
- [ ]  User Outcomes describe **real value**

# 📝 Summary

The **Lean UX Canvas** provides a structured way to articulate problems, success measures, and value for users. By completing Sections 1–4, students clarify the foundation of their research and design work.
