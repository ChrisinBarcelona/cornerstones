# Solution Sketch

# 🪜 Stage: Ideation

One idea, detailed enough to explain itself.

# 👋 Context

After Crazy 8's, everyone has lots of rough ideas. A **solution sketch** turns your best one into a detailed concept that the team can understand without you in the room. In the Design Sprint it is the fourth step of the "4-step sketch" (notes, ideas, Crazy 8's, solution sketch).

It's a sketch, not a drawing competition. What matters is that the idea is clear: boxes, arrows and words are enough. The sketches are then hung up like an art gallery and presented, which kicks off the Decide phase.

# 🎯 Goal / Learning Outcome

- Develop your strongest idea into a detailed, three-panel storyboard.
- Make the sketch self-explanatory, with real words instead of squiggles.
- Present the concept clearly in a few minutes.
- Prepare the ideas for discussion and voting.

# 🛠️ Main Exercise

1. **Pick your best idea (5 min).** Look back at your Crazy 8's and choose the one you'd bet on.
2. **Sketch it in three panels (30 min, alone).** Use three sticky notes or an A4 sheet split in three, like a storyboard: what the user sees first, what they do next, and what happens at the end.
3. **Use real words.** Write actual headlines, button labels and content. Words carry most of the idea.
4. **Give it a catchy title.** E.g. "Tap & Lock" or "Swim Pass". It makes the idea easy to refer to later.
5. **Hang it up (art gallery).** Put all the sketches on the wall, side by side, like paintings in a museum.
6. **Present (3–5 min each).** Walk the team through your sketch. Others can ask questions about the details.

# ⏱️ Estimated Duration

- ~1 hour

# 🏞️ Template

| Panel 1 | Panel 2 | Panel 3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The first thing the user sees | What they do next | The result |

Plus: a catchy title at the top.

# ⚡ Tips

- Ugly is fine; unclear is not.
- Keep it anonymous if you can (no names on the sketches), so ideas are judged, not people.
- Show one flow, not the whole app.
- If you can't explain it in three panels, simplify the idea.

# ✨ AI Assist

Describe your concept to Claude and ask: "What would confuse a first-time user in this flow?" Fix the sketch before you hang it up, not after.
