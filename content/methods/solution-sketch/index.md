# Solution Sketch

Let’s kick off the Decide phase of a Design Sprint by having everyone present their Solution Sketch to the class and discuss the various features of the concept. 

So with that being said, let’s give you guys a moment to present.

![wireframes.jpg](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1581291519195-ef11498d1cf2?ixlib=rb-4.0.3&q=85&fm=jpg&crop=entropy&cs=srgb)

1. Hang everyone’s sketches up on a wall in the same way art is presented in a gallery or museum
2. Each person has three to five minutes to present their solution
3. The team can ask questions or discuss details in the sketch
