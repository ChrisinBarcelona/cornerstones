# Brand Pack

# 🪜 Stage: Ideation

Turn the mood into a brand: name, logo, colour, type and voice.

# 👋 Context

Your mood board captured a feeling. A **brand pack** turns that feeling into a small set of decisions that everyone on the team can use: a name and logo, a colour palette, typefaces, an image style and a tone of voice.

It sits between the mood board and the style guide:

- **Mood board:** the feeling, in pictures. Loose and exploratory.
- **Brand pack:** the identity. A few clear choices, each with a reason.
- **Style guide / design system:** the rules for the interface: buttons, spacing, components.

Nielsen Norman Group's work on visual design and tone of voice is a useful reminder that brand isn't decoration: colour, type and words shape how trustworthy, friendly or serious a product feels, and those choices should come from your users and your mood words, not just personal taste.

# 🎯 Goal / Learning Outcome

- Translate mood words into concrete brand decisions.
- Build a primary and secondary colour palette that works on screen and passes contrast.
- Choose typefaces and an image style that support the brand personality.
- Define a tone of voice with examples of how the product speaks.

# 🛠️ Main Exercise

Build it on one Figma page.

1. **Brand personality.** Start from your 4–5 mood words. Write one sentence: "We are ___, not ___." (e.g. "Warm, not childish.")
2. **Name and logo.** A working name and a simple logo or wordmark, in colour and in one colour.
3. **Colour palette.** One primary colour, one or two secondary colours, neutrals, and colours for success/warning/error. Check text contrast.
4. **Typography.** A heading typeface and a body typeface, with a simple scale (H1, H2, body, caption).
5. **Imagery and icons.** Three example images that show the photo or illustration style, and the icon style (outline, filled, rounded…).
6. **Tone of voice.** Place your brand on NN/g's four dimensions: **funny ↔ serious**, **formal ↔ casual**, **respectful ↔ irreverent**, **enthusiastic ↔ matter-of-fact**. Write a welcome message and an error message in that voice.
7. **Do's and don'ts.** Two or three of each, so others use the brand correctly.

# ⏱️ Estimated Duration

- ~2 hours

# 🏞️ Template

- 🧭 Personality ("We are ___, not ___")
- 🔤 Name and logo
- 🎨 Colour palette
- 🔠 Typography
- 🖼️ Imagery and icon style
- 💬 Tone of voice, with examples
- ✅ Do's and ❌ Don'ts

# ⚡ Tips

- Fewer, stronger choices. Two typefaces and a tight palette are plenty.
- Every choice should trace back to a mood word or a user insight.
- Test colours on real UI (a button, a card), not just as swatches.
- Free fonts on Google Fonts are fine; check the licence for anything else.

# ✨ AI Assist

Give Claude your mood words and persona and ask for three different tone-of-voice directions, each with a welcome and an error message. Pick one, then rewrite it in your own words.

# 📝 Summary

A **brand pack** turns the mood of a project into a usable identity: name, logo, colours, type, imagery and voice. Each decision is small, deliberate and tied back to the users and the mood words, so the whole team can design and write with one consistent personality.
