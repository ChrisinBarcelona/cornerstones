Minimum Viable Product

The leanest possible version of your product that could go to market and still answer your research question.

The essential ingredients only. No embellishments, no bonus features.

Most Valuable Person

The user segment that brings the most value to the business, product or service.

  • Minimum Desirable Product What one extra would make them love it?

How to cut it down

  1. List every feature you could build
  2. Name your Most Valuable Person in one sentence
  3. Cross out everything that person doesn't need on day one. What's left is your MVP
  4. Add back the one feature that would make them love it. That's your MDP

Cut it down

Paseo is a dog-walking app for busy professionals in Barcelona. The team wants:

Book a walker · Live GPS of the walk · Walker reviews · In-app payment · Chat with the walker · Dog social network · Photo diary of each walk · Vet booking · Loyalty points · Dog-food shop

Who's the Most Valuable Person? What's the MVP? What's the one MDP extra?

UX Deck · Target MVP9 / 10

One possible answer

  • Most Valuable Person: a professional who works long office hours and worries about their dog alone at home
  • MVP: book a walker, walker reviews and in-app payment. Enough to test whether people will pay a stranger to walk their dog
  • MDP extra: a photo from each walk. It speaks straight to the worry, and it's cheap to build
  • Later, maybe: live GPS and chat. Cut: the social network, vet booking, loyalty points and shop. Each is a product of its own
