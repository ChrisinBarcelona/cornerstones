Sprint Toolkit

UX Methods

Your bank of 46 methods for different types of problems. Pick and choose: there's nothing to complete here, and no sprint uses them all. Find the stage you're at, then the method that fits your problem. Each one opens with what it's for and how long it takes.

Start with Lean UX Canvas
Category
Showing 46 of 46
Time

  1. Discovery

    Generative research: find out what the problem really is, and who has it.

  2. Lean UX Canvas✓ Done

    Frame the problem as a team: the business outcome, the users and the value they'll get.

    25–30 min
  3. Long Term Goal✓ Done

    Agree the best-case future for your users, so every sprint decision has a direction.

  4. Research Goal✓ Done

    Write down what you want to learn before you start researching.

    30 min
  5. Research Questions✓ Done

    Turn your research goal into specific, open questions that guide the research.

    30 min
  6. Desk Research✓ Done

    Gather what is already known about your problem from credible sources, then decide what your own research must find out.

    2.5 hr
  7. Site Visit✓ Done

    Go to the place where the problem happens and observe quietly, to pick up cues users would never mention.

    1.5 hr
  8. User Survey✓ Done

    Design an unbiased survey to gather evidence from people's real experiences.

    50–80 min
  9. User Interview✓ Done

    Interview users to uncover the why behind what they do and need.

    In stages
  10. Discovery Summary✓ Done

    Pull your research together into key insights, surprises and next steps.

    60 min

  11. Define

    Turn research into a clear problem, a target user and a direction.

  12. Affinity Mapping✓ Done

    Sort research findings into clusters on sticky notes, name the themes and agree what matters most.

    1 hr
  13. Data Synthesis with AI✓ Done

    Use AI for a first pass at coding your research notes, then check every suggestion against the raw data.

    1.5 hr
  14. Problem Statement✓ Done

    Write a short, solution-free description of the main problem users face.

    10–15 min
  15. User Persona✓ Done

    Turn your research into an evidence-based persona of your target user.

    55 min
  16. Competitive Analysis✓ Done

    Study 3–5 competitors to find gaps and ideas for your design.

    30–40 min
  17. Business Model Canvas✓ Done

    Describe how the idea creates, delivers and captures value in nine blocks on one page.

    1 hr
  18. Wicked Problem Mapping✓ Done

    Map a wicked problem as a web of causes and actors, then find one leverage point small enough to design for.

    1.5 hr
  19. How Might We✓ Done

    List the ways your project could fail, then flip them into 'How might we…' opportunities.

  20. Pick a Target✓ Done

    Choose your Minimum Viable Product, Most Valuable Person and Minimum Desirable Product.

    40 min
  21. User Journey Map✓ Done

    Map the user's steps, feelings and pain points as they work towards a goal.

    55 min
  22. Service Design Blueprint✓ Done

    Map one journey from the customer's actions down to the backstage processes that make it happen.

    1.5 hr
  23. DVF Analysis✓ Done

    Score ideas for desirability, viability and feasibility, then rank and discuss them as a team.

    45 min
  24. Converge / Diverge✓ Done

    Decide as a team whether to combine ideas into one prototype or test competing ones.

  25. Vote✓ Done

    Vote on the sketches to choose which ideas to take forward.

  26. Define › Information Architecture

    Part of Define: structure the steps users take to reach their goal.

  27. Task Flows✓ Done

    Break the main task into the fewest steps a user needs, drawn as a simple flow.

    15 min
  28. Sitemap✓ Done

    Draw the hierarchy of every page in your site or app, and check it for gaps and pages buried too deep.

    1 hr

  29. Ideation

    Diverge on many ideas, decide on one, then design it from paper to high fidelity.

  30. Crazy 8’s✓ Done

    Sketch eight different ideas in eight minutes to get past your first idea.

    15 min
  31. Crazy 8’s with AI✓ Done

    Crazy 8's with AI as a sparring partner, suggesting variations for you to sketch.

    15 min
  32. Solution Sketch✓ Done

    Present your solution sketch to the team, gallery-style, and discuss it.

  33. Mood Board✓ Done

    Collect colours, type, imagery and textures to explore the look and feel.

    1 hr
  34. Mood Board with AI✓ Done

    Generate, react and refine visuals with AI in loops, then build a mood board around your mood words.

    1 hr
  35. Brand Pack✓ Done

    Turn your mood board into an identity: name, logo, colour, type, imagery and tone of voice.

    2 hr
  36. Style Guide✓ Done

    Turn your mood board into a practical guide: logo, type, colours, icons and buttons.

    2 hr
  37. Paper Wireframes✓ Done

    Sketch your main task flow on paper, focusing on structure, not polish.

    45 min
  38. Medium Fidelity (Mid-Fi)✓ Done

    Rebuild your paper wireframes as greyscale mid-fi screens with the Figma Mid-Fi kit or Miro, only for the screens you need.

    2 hr
  39. Hi-Fi✓ Done

    Turn your wireframes into a polished, clickable Figma prototype with real content.

    3 hr

  40. Validation

    Test the prototype with real users and iterate on what you learn.

  41. Usability Test✓ Done

    Plan and run five usability tests, then find the patterns in what users did.

    In stages
  42. Create your Script✓ Done

    Write and rehearse the script you'll follow when testing with users.

  43. Facilitator Format✓ Done

    How the facilitator runs a test: welcome, context, objective, thinking aloud and the debrief.

  44. Limited Intervention Technique✓ Done

    Don't lead users during a test: give clues, not answers.

  45. Notetaker Format✓ Done

    What the note-taker watches for: what users say and do, and what they think and feel.

  46. A/B Test✓ Done

    Compare two versions of a screen that differ in one way, on task success and preference, and decide with evidence.

    1.5 hr
  47. Design Iteration✓ Done

    Turn test findings into 2–3 design changes, shown as before-and-after snapshots.

    1 hr

  48. Retrospective

    Reflect on the sprint and capture the lessons for your case study.

  49. Sprint Retrospective✓ Done

    Reflect on what worked, what didn't and what you learned. It becomes the first page of your case study.

    In stages
  50. Cataloguing Artifacts✓ Done

    Gather, label and sort every artifact from your projects, and choose the strongest for your case studies.

    3 hr
  51. Process Narrative✓ Done

    Write a case study as a story in four beats: purpose, challenges, when things got tough, and reflection.

    3 hr
  52. Building your Web Portfolio✓ Done

    Build your portfolio website in Framer: a two-week plan from setup and styles to a CMS case-study template, with the videos for each phase.

    5 days
