Definition

What is a Discovery Summary? A short write-up at the end of discovery that pulls all the research together into the key insights, patterns and surprises that will shape the design.

When
Discovery: Generative research: find out what the problem really is, and who has it.

👋Context

Congratulations on completing all your research for discovery and now it’s time to officially wrap on our generative phase. This point marks the completion of our work here and now it’s time to capture the main findings.

Think of this step as taking a snapshot of everything you’ve learned so far — the insights that will guide your next steps in design or investigation.

Purpose Questions to Ask Yourself Documentation Method
Capture the key takeaways from your research. What patterns or trends did you notice across your data? Notes, sticky notes, or a shared document summarising insights.
Identify surprises or unexpected findings. Did anything contradict your assumptions? Highlight or annotate quotes, survey results, or observations.
Connect discoveries to your research goal. Which insights are most relevant to your original goal? Create a “top insights” list linking back to research questions.
Determine next steps. What unanswered questions remain? What should you explore further? Brainstorm follow-up questions or areas for deeper investigation.

🎯Goal / Learning outcome

By the end of this activity, you will be able to synthesise your discovery research into a clear, organised summary that highlights insights, patterns, and next steps.

🛠️Main Exercise

  1. Design the story!
  2. Review all your notes, surveys, interviews, and observations from the discovery phase.
  3. Identify patterns, surprises, and key takeaways.
  4. For each insight, ask yourself:
    • Why is this important?
    • How does it connect to my research goal?
    • What new questions does it raise?
  5. Create a Discovery Summary like the one below:
Insight / Finding Why it matters Evidence / Observation Next Steps / What’s missing?
  1. Reflect on your summary: Are there any gaps? Does this give a clear picture of your findings?
Extra Notes
  • Avoid simply listing raw data; focus on what it means and the story!@
  • Include both positive and negative findings — contradictions are valuable.
Resources
  • Question prompts:
    • “What surprised me?”
    • “Which insights connect directly to my research goal?”

Summary

Your Discovery Summary is your snapshot of understanding — a distilled view of insights that sets the stage for ideation or further exploration. It ensures that the generative work you’ve done leads directly into informed action.
