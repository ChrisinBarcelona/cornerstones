The brief

Design the first-run onboarding for Mercat, an app that lets you order from Barcelona's neighbourhood market stalls and pick up on your way home. A new user has just downloaded it. In a few screens, show them why it's worth using, get the one permission the app really needs, and get them to their first stall as fast as possible.

Good onboarding is short. Every extra screen is a chance to lose someone, so every screen you add has to earn its place.

Deliverable

  • Three to four onboarding screens, in iPhone 16 frames (393 × 852) in Figma, on the 8-point grid.
  • The screens connected as a clickable prototype, including Skip.
  • Extra challenge: the first home screen the user lands on after onboarding.

Must include

  1. A clear value proposition on the first screen
  2. Progress indicator (dots or steps)
  3. Skip option on every screen
  4. A permission request (location, to find the nearest market) that explains why before the system prompt
  5. A primary call to action that ends onboarding

Nice to have

  • Choose your market or neighbourhood
  • Pick favourite stalls or food types to personalise the home screen
  • Language choice (Català, Castellano, English)
  • Sign in with Apple or Google, or "Continue as guest"
  • A small illustration or photo per screen in a consistent style

Inspiration

Search for onboarding on Mobbin and compare how three food or delivery apps start. Note which ones ask for permissions up front and which wait until they're needed.

Mobile-App Onboarding: An Analysis of Components and Techniques – NN/g

Time box

Time box: half a day (about 4 hours)

Before you submit

☐ Frames are iPhone 16 (393 × 852) and spacing follows the 8-point grid

☐ Every Must include item is on the screens

☐ No more than four screens, and Skip works on every one

☐ The permission screen says why before it asks

☐ Text is readable and colour contrast passes (4.5:1 for body text)

☐ Buttons and dots are components; layers are named and grouped

☐ Shared the Figma prototype link and a PNG export with your instructor
