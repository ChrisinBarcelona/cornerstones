Sidebar

As we are learning, good orientation is a staple of the UX diet. How users move about from page to page is a cornerstone of good UI. Let’s imagine you are designing a simple sidebar rail. You’ll want to consider individual options like; Make a new entry, search my topics, send my story, change colour scheme stuff like that! Good Luck!
