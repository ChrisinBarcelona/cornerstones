The brief

You've just joined Flicker, a film streaming service, as a UI designer. Design the video player: the controls people use while a film is playing. Good player controls are simple, recognisable and need no thought; they're universally understood.

Source: https://dribbble.com/rebeccanoren

Deliverable

  • The player in an iPhone 16 frame (393 × 852) in Figma, on the 8-point grid, in landscape (852 × 393), with the controls showing.

Must include

  1. Play / pause
  2. Timeline with current time and duration
  3. Volume
  4. Full screen
  5. Subtitles and language

Nice to have

  • Film details
  • Rating
  • Coming next
  • Credits and contributors

Inspiration

Source: https://dribbble.com/uxmadhuridesign

Source: https://dribbble.com/am-designer

Time box

Time box: 3 hours

Before you submit

☐ Frame is iPhone 16 (393 × 852) and spacing follows the 8-point grid

☐ Every Must include item is on the screen

☐ Controls are readable over both bright and dark video

☐ Touch targets are at least 44 × 44 pt

☐ Text is readable and colour contrast passes (4.5:1 for body text)

☐ Layers are named and grouped; repeated elements are components

☐ Shared the Figma link and a PNG export with your instructor
