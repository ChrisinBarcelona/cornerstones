Design a Product Card for Wallpaper

Design Challenge

Design a product card that effectively showcases a wallpaper product within an online shopping or editorial environment. The card should communicate essential product information at a glance while feeling visually rich, brand-consistent, and easy to interact with.

Think about how your design can balance aesthetic appeal with clarity and usability — the card should make the wallpaper feel tactile, desirable, and easy to explore, while aligning with users’ existing expectations of e-commerce layouts.

Potential Inclusions

  • Product Name — clear and prominent title (e.g., “Marble Mist Wallpaper”).
  • SKU / Product Code — smaller type for reference or ordering.
  • Price / Sale Price — primary callout, possibly with a discount or “on sale” badge.
  • Add to Cart / Buy Button — clear action button for purchase.
  • Share Button — option to share via social or copy link.
  • Main Product Image — high-resolution wallpaper preview.
  • Gallery / Thumbnail Images — secondary images showing texture, close-ups, or styled interiors.
  • Product Details — key specs like material, roll dimensions, repeat size, and finish.
  • Care Instructions — short note on maintenance or cleaning.
  • “You Might Also Like” Section — related wallpapers or complementary colors/patterns.
  • Favorite / Wishlist Icon — heart or bookmark toggle.
